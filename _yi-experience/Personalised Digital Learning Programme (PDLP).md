<p><strong>How will your child/ward use their Personal Learning Device (PLD)?</strong>
</p>
<p>At YISS, your child/ward will be using the PLD for</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Curation and research</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Creation of digital artefacts</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Collaboration with peers&nbsp;</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Learning through various EdTech platforms during lessons</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p>Our school uses&nbsp;<strong>Apple iPads</strong> as the PLD for all students<strong>.</strong>
</p>
<p>Students receive different iPad models depending on their year of admission
to the school. Each iPad comes with a case, keyboard, Apple Pencil, and
3–year warranty and insurance (except the 2026 cohort, which has 4-year
warranty and insurance).</p>
