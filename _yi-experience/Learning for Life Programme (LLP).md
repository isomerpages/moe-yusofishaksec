<h1><strong>Learning for Life Programme (LLP)</strong></h1>
<h3>Nurturing Honourable Leaders for Tomorrow (YI LEAD+)</h3>
<p>An honourable LEADER in every Yusoffian. We nurture honourable leaders
for tomorrow who are grounded in the core values of Honourable Leaders,
way makers who inspire social action, possess a global outlook and adopt
a growth mind-set.</p>
<p>Our LLP serves to broaden students’ global outlook and deepen students’
acquisition of 21st Century Competencies (21CC) to nurture them to be Leaders
for Tomorrow. Through a comprehensive LLP programme, our student leaders
will be empowered to be of positive influence to their peers and juniors,
and the community, becoming Leaders for the Greater Good.</p>
<h4><strong>Key programmes that Develop Leaders for Tomorrow</strong></h4>
<p>Through various training, authentic leadership experiences and guided
reflection, student leaders strengthen their leadership capacity and deepen
their acquisition of 21<sup>st</sup> Century Competencies (21CC).</p>
<p>These programmes emphasise communication, collaboration, critical thinking,
global awareness and ethical decision-making. By engaging in leadership
training workshops, combined camps, dialogue sessions, and opportunities
to conceptualise and execute school-based initiatives, students build the
foundational skills required to lead with gratitude, respect, integrity
and teamwork.</p>
<p>Through these experiences, they are equipped not only with competence,
but also with character, and nurtured to become Leaders for Tomorrow.</p>

<h4><strong>Key programmes that Inspire Leaders for the Greater Good</strong></h4>
<p>Throughout the year, our student leaders apply the skills and values developed
through their training to serve their peers, juniors and the wider community.
These programmes include school-wide service projects as well as community
outreach initiatives.</p>
<p>By leading beyond themselves and contributing meaningfully to others by
using their influence to uplift and positively impact the community, students
embody the spirit of Leaders for the Greater Good.</p>
