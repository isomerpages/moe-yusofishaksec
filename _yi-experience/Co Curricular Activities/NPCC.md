<p>The National Police Cadet Corps (NPCC) unit at Yusof Ishak Secondary School
develops cadets into leaders for the greater good through the inculcation
of leadership, discipline, and service-oriented mindsets and values via
structured training, community engagement, and teamwork. Cadets will learn
police knowledge and drills, participate in ceremonial duties, support
school events, and serve the community through outreach and service projects.
YI NPCC builds resilience, confidence, and integrity while fostering a
spirit of responsibility and civic commitment among cadets.</p>
<h4><strong>Key Programmes</strong></h4>
<p><strong>a)&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; <u>Adventure Training Camp:</u></strong>
</p>
<p>Camp Resilience at Pulau Ubin offers our Secondary 2 cadets a transformative
three-day outdoor adventure experience. The Annual Training Camp immerses
cadets in an authentic outdoors learning experience where they develop
resilience, teamwork, and leadership through outdoor challenges, team-building,
and scenario-based activities.</p>

<p><strong>b)&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; <u>Inter-Unit and Area Events</u></strong>
</p>
<p>Inter-Unit and Area events, such as the NPCC Annual Parade and Area Games
Day, offer cadets opportunities to interact with peers from some other
schools in the Northeast region. Cadets participate in joint training and
competitions which broadens their exposure to NPCC beyond the school environment
and enables them to learn and share best practices.</p>

<p><strong>c)&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; <u>Courses and Badge Programmes</u></strong>
</p>
<p>Through courses and badge programmes, cadets acquire competencies and
knowledge such as leadership, first aid, and unarmed tactics while working
towards recognized NPCC badges that support their personal growth and rank
progression.</p>
