<p>In our school's table tennis programme, students develop both athletic
excellence and strong character through the regular training sessions and
exposure to competitive events. Students will also have opportunities to
hone their leadership skills by being involved in different committees
that serve to enhance the learning experience within the CCA.</p>
<h4><strong>Key Programmes</strong></h4>
<p><strong><u>National School Games (NSG)</u></strong>
</p>
<p>Through competitive play, our students develop technical skills, strategic
thinking and greater appreciation of teamwork as they work together to
seek improvements for themselves and others.</p>
