<p>The STEM Club caters to students who show greater aptitude and/or interest
in STEM (Science, Technology, Engineering &amp; Math). We want to deepen
STEM Learning &amp; Values, equip students with the skills and dispositions
to be <strong>Leaders for Tomorrow</strong>: Lateral Thinkers, Ethical,
Advocates, and Digital Innovators. We provide a platform for students to
Engage &amp; Explore STEM through themes such as Biodiversity, Sustainability,
Aeronautics and Engineering and Empower them to innovate and contribute
to the Greater Good.</p>
<h4><strong>Key Programmes</strong></h4>
<p><strong>a)&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; <u>NPARKS Biodiversity Workshop</u></strong>
</p>
<p>Our students learn about how to identify different plant and animal species
that can be found in our school and appreciate and support local biodiversity.</p>
