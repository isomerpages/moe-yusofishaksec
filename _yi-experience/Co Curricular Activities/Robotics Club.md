<p><strong>YI Robotics Club</strong> offers a hands-on STEM experience that
develops students into confident problem-solvers and innovators through
robotics and coding. Anchored in <strong>LEGO SPIKE Robotics (CSARC-certified)</strong>, <strong>Python programming</strong>,
and <strong>manual and autonomous drone flight</strong>, the programme equips
students with strong foundations in computational thinking, engineering
design, and real-world application of technology, while fostering teamwork,
resilience, and responsible use of emerging technologies.</p>
<h4><strong>Key Programmes</strong></h4>
<p><strong>a)&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; <u>LEGO Spike Robotics with CSARC Certification&nbsp;</u></strong>
</p>
<p>Our students go from basic coding to hands-on robotics programme developing
engineering design, problem-solving, and computational thinking through
the use of LEGO SPIKE with CSARC-aligned certification.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p></p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p><strong>b)&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; <u>Drone Flying (Manual &amp; Automated)</u></strong>
</p>
<p>Students develop in the aerial robotics programme where students learn
safe manual piloting and program autonomous drone flights using block-based
tools and Python.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p></p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p><strong>c)&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; <u>Python Programming</u></strong>
</p>
<p>Students explore progressive text-based coding programme that equip students
with Python skills for robotics control, automation, and advanced logical
thinking.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
