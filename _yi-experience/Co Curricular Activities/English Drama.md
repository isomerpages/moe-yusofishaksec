<p><strong>English Drama Club</strong>
</p>
<p>The English Drama Club is a vibrant community of storytellers, performers,
and technicians. We believe that theatre is more than just "acting", it
is a journey of self-discovery, teamwork, and building the confidence to
lead.</p>
<p><strong>Key Programmes</strong>
</p>
<p><strong>a)&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; <u>Singapore Youth Festival (SYF)</u></strong>
</p>
<p>The English Drama Club actively participates in SYF Arts Presentation,
a biennial showcase of excellence in the performing arts. We attained the
Certificate of Distinction in 2025 after months of rehearsal.</p>

<p><strong>c)&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; <u>School Exchange @ St Andrew’s Secondary School</u></strong>
</p>
<p>In 2025, we took part in an exchange programme at St Andrew’s Secondary
School, during which we exchanged ideas and received constructive feedback
on our craft and performance piece. Through this experience, we gained
a clearer understanding of how our artistic choices impacted the overall
performance and how we can improve them.</p>
