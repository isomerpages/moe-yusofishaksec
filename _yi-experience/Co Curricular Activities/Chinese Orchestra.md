<p>The Yusof Ishak Chinese Orchestra (YICO) provides students with a comprehensive
platform to learn, perform, and appreciate Chinese orchestral music. Through
guided instruction and regular rehearsals, members gain exposure to a wide
range of traditional Chinese instruments and repertoire, developing strong
technical skills and musical understanding.</p>
<p>Alongside classical works, the orchestra also performs contemporary and
Western pop pieces adapted for Chinese instruments, allowing students to
experience a broad spectrum of musical styles. As part of their learning
journey, YICO members attend live performances by other Chinese orchestras.
These experiences enable students to observe different interpretations
and performance practices, deepening their understanding of Chinese music
and encouraging thoughtful reflection on their own musicianship.</p>
<p>Through ensemble work and performances, members cultivate teamwork, discipline,
and leadership skills while building lasting friendships in a supportive
environment. With a commitment to cultural appreciation and community engagement,
the orchestra regularly performs at key school events and represents the
school at external platforms including exchange programmes and the Singapore
Youth Festival (SYF).</p>
<h4><strong>Key Programmes</strong></h4>
<p><strong>(a)&nbsp;&nbsp; Inter-School Chinese Orchestra Music Exchange</strong>
</p>
<p>YICO participated in the 8th Inter-School Chinese Orchestra Music Exchange,
organized by the Keat Hong Chinese Orchestra. The event aimed to deepen
youths’ appreciation of Chinese music and provided a platform for Chinese
Orchestras from 11 schools across Singapore to learn from one another,
refine their skills, and showcase their musical talents.&nbsp;</p>

<p><strong>(b)&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Singapore Youth Festival Arts Presentation</strong>
</p>
<p>YICO participated in the Singapore Youth Festival Arts Presentation after
months of focused preparation. Under the guidance of conductor Mr. Goh
Wei Hong, members presented two selected works that highlighted their technical
competence, musical sensitivity, and ensemble unity. The experience offered
valuable performance exposure, reinforced discipline and teamwork, and
reflected the orchestra’s ongoing commitment to high artistic standards
and continual growth.</p>

<p><strong>(c)&nbsp;&nbsp; School Events and Concerts</strong>
</p>
<p>YICO also takes part in a wide range of school performances, including
Speech Day, Chinese New Year celebrations, and Performing Arts concert.
Through these platforms, members apply the skills developed during rehearsals,
learning to perform confidently before an audience, work closely as an
ensemble, and adapt to different performance settings. These experiences
help students develop stage presence, musical discipline, and a strong
sense of responsibility.</p>
