<p>Art Club is a diverse community of artists and art-lovers who embrace
the unique art styles of each student. Students in Art Club are introduced
to a diverse range of art forms and media across traditional and digital
art. We focus on strengthening artistic skills, visual story-telling and
developing the imagination. Students learn<strong> </strong>through<strong> play, experimentation, drawing and reflection</strong>.</p>
