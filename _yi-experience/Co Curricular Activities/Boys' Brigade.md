<p>The Boys' Brigade CCA in Yusof Ishak Secondary is also known as 1st Singapore
Company. We seek to develop our boys to grow in character, to become responsible
citizens, to improve physical fitness and develop their leadership potential.</p>
<p>This is achieved through a range of activities covering the four domains
of Physical, Enrichment, Spiritual, and Social.</p>
<p>With the support of our committed Teachers and Officers, our boys will
mature to become men who are sure and steadfast, ready to be leaders for
tomorrow.</p>
<h4><strong>Key Programmes</strong></h4>
<p><strong>a)&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; <u>Adventure Camp:</u></strong>
</p>
<p>A 2D1N camp that promotes the spirit of adventure and discovery through
expedition and campcraft practiced in an adventure setting. Highlights
include field cooking, pitching of improvised shelter and navigation.</p>
<p><strong>b)&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; <u>BB CARES:</u></strong>
</p>
<p>Boys organize and interact with beneficiaries from Bright Hill Evergreen
Home where they grow through community service.</p>
