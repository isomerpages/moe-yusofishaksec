<p>In Choir, we support one another and work hard together, growing in confidence
through every rehearsal and performance. United by our voices, we strive
for excellence while building strong friendships and experiencing the joy
of singing together. Under the guidance of our instructor, we learn and
celebrate every step of our musical journey as one choir.</p>
<h4><strong>Key Programmes</strong></h4>
<p><strong>a)&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; <u>Singapore Youth Festival (SYF)</u></strong>
</p>
<p>Our Choir actively participates in the SYF Arts Presentation, a national
platform that celebrates students’ artistic growth and musical development.
Through SYF, students learn discipline, resilience, and teamwork. Our choir
has achieved the Accomplishment, recognizing a strong and proficient performance.</p>

<p><strong>b)&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; <u>Bi-Annual Concerts</u></strong>
</p>
<p>Our concerts provide a meaningful platform for students to showcase their
talents and hard work. Students take ownership of the performances, developing
confidence, leadership, and a strong sense of pride in representing the
choir.</p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">

<p><strong>c)&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; <u>Choir Exchange Programmes</u></strong>
</p>
<p>Through choir exchanges, our students collaborate and bond with choirs
from other schools. These exchanges broaden musical exposure, encourage
cultural sharing, and build friendships beyond our school community, including
exchanges with Pei Hwa Secondary School and Nanyang Junior College.</p>
