<p>The Yusof Ishak Secondary School Concert Band is a group of passionate
students who enjoy learning and making music together. Consisting of instruments
from the Woodwind, Brass and Percussion sections, the Concert Band presents
music of various genres and styles. Our Concert Band students not only
grow to be artistic musicians, but also to be resilient and self-motivated
learners.</p>
<h4><strong>Key Programmes</strong></h4>
<p><strong>a)&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; <u>Singapore Youth Festival Arts Presentation (SYFAP) and Public Performances</u></strong>
</p>
<p>The Concert Band actively participates in the SYF Arts Presentation, a
signature platform celebrating students' artistic growth. Furthermore,
the Concert Band also has opportunities to perform at public events, such
as the Pasir-Ris Punggol National Day Dinner in August 2024. Through participation
in the SYF Arts Presentation and public performances, our Concert Band
students grow in confidence and character through the arts.</p>

<p><strong>b)&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; <u>School Events and National Day Celebrations</u></strong>
</p>
<p>The Concert Band is proud to perform at various school events, for example
during our annual Speech Days. Every year, the Concert Band also plays
an important part in our National Day celebrations in school, providing
live accompaniment for the march-in of the uniform groups, as well as our
national anthem. This aims to foster national identity and pride through
music.&nbsp;</p>
