<p>In our school's dynamic and vibrant basketball programme, students develop
both athletic excellence and strong character through a comprehensive training
plan that helps instill in them a strong sense of belonging and discipline.
CCA Teachers serve as mentors to the student athletes, and we hope that
over their time in the CCA, students are able become a better version of
themselves and serve to be good role models to their peers and juniors.</p>
<p>Through this programme, we also aim to develop their leadership qualities
and sportsmanship. Student athletes are also encouraged to take self-ownership
of the team via planning and execution of training activities as well as
day-to-day duties.</p>
<h4><strong>Key Programmes</strong></h4>
<p>National School B’ Division Zonal Basketball Championship (January till
March/April)
<br>Nationals&nbsp; C’ Division League Basketball Championship (April - May,
July - August)</p>
