<p>Our Modern Dance Team is a vibrant family of passionate dancers who represent
our school through the art of movement. Our CCA allows dancers to explore
a wide range of genres, including contemporary, hip hop/street dance, jazz,
ballet and more, nurturing versatile and well-rounded performers. The Modern
Dance Team strives to continuously build a strong repertoire of both Hip
Hop and Contemporary techniques, equipping students with diverse skills
and expressive abilities. With passion and a deep appreciation for the
arts, our dancers face challenges with resilience, using dance as a powerful
tool of self-expression. Through this journey, they develop confidence,
teamwork, and strong communication skills, learning to care for and support
one another as one dance family while forging meaningful bonds and lasting
friendships.</p>

<p>Our Modern Dance Team actively participates in the SYF Arts Presentation,
a signature national platform celebrating students’ artistic growth, creativity,
and excellence in the arts. Through this journey, dancers are guided in
choreography interpretation, technical refinement, stage discipline, and
expressive storytelling. The SYF experience nurtures perseverance, professionalism,
and artistic maturity, as dancers engage in structured rehearsals and reflective
learning. We proudly obtained a <strong>Distinction</strong> in our most
recent SYF performance in <strong>2025</strong>, reflecting our commitment
to high standards, teamwork, and continuous improvement.</p>

<p>
<br>Our Modern Dance Team actively participates in bi-annual concerts, school
events, and external competitions such as Super24, providing dancers with
regular opportunities to perform, grow, and showcase their talents. These
platforms expose students to diverse performance settings and audiences,
helping them build stage presence, confidence, adaptability, and resilience.
Through performances and competitions, dancers develop discipline, teamwork,
and professionalism, while learning to support one another and represent
the school with pride, excellence, and sportsmanship.</p>
