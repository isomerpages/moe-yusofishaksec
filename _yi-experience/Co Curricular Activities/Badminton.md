<p>Our Badminton CCA aims to nurture well-rounded sportsmen and athletes
who excel not only in badminton skills but also in character. Through our
weekly training programme, students learn essential technical skills required
of a badminton player, while also developing important life skills such
as resilience, grit, and discipline.</p>
<p>The teachers-in-charge and coaches are dedicated to nurturing students
holistically, focusing on both their physical development and character
growth.</p>
<p>We believe that the challenges faced in a sports CCA play a vital role
in shaping students to demonstrate the school values of Gratitude, Respect,
Integrity, and Teamwork. Students are also provided with leadership opportunities,
allowing them to grow into confident and responsible individuals.</p>
<h4><strong>Key Programmes</strong></h4>
<p><strong><u>National School Games (NSG):</u> </strong>Through competitive
play, our students develop technical skills, strategic thinking and resilience.
The Badminton CCA students demonstrated their commitment to excellence
when they prepared for the National School Games the year before. The regular
practice combining drills, footwork, and agility exercises coupled with
constant reflection help to prepare the students for the rigours of the
tournament. &nbsp;The students also learnt many life skills such as communication,
sportsmanship and Fair Play. This commitment by the students to excel in
the National School Games have helped them to build up their confidence
in themselves and in their own abilities.</p>
